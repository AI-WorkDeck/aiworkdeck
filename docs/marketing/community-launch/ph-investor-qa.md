# Product Hunt supplementary project background

This replaces the September 2026 investor questionnaire draft. It is project background, not a statement of current fundraising, revenue, pricing, or adoption figures.

AI WorkDeck is an open-source exploration rooted in legal practice, undertaken outside working hours with team support and feedback from colleagues. Its initiator, Zewei Han, is a practicing lawyer and partner at Beijing Redsunny Law. He previously worked at King & Wood Mallesons and supported the firm’s legal technology team on projects. He co-authored 《大模型时代的法律科技》.

The work focuses on bringing project files, document editing, AI assistance, references, and version history into a shared workspace. Colleagues’ feedback helps identify practical problems and assess whether proposed changes are useful. The project shares this work with practitioners and developers so they can inspect, adapt, and improve it.
