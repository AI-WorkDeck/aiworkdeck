## Name

AI WorkDeck

## Tagline

Open-source AI workspace shaped by everyday legal practice

## Description

AI WorkDeck is an open-source workspace shaped by everyday legal work and colleagues' feedback. Developed outside client work with team support, it brings documents, project files, AI assistance and version history together. Review AI edits as tracked changes and check linked sources. An exploration shared with the profession under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

## Maker comment

Hi Product Hunt — I'm Zewei, a practicing lawyer and a partner at Beijing Redsunny Law. Previously, I worked at King & Wood Mallesons, where I supported projects with the legal technology team. I also co-authored a book on legal technology in the era of large language models.

AI WorkDeck grew out of everyday legal work and conversations with colleagues: reviewing a proposed clause, checking the material behind a finding, and keeping track of revisions across a matter. Those practical needs and colleagues' feedback continue to shape the project.

This is an open-source exploration developed in my spare time, with support from my team, to share what we learn with the profession. The focus is on bringing AI assistance into the workspace where documents are prepared and reviewed, while keeping professional judgment with the people responsible for the work.

The workspace brings together project files, an embedded LibreOffice-based editor, AI conversation and version history. AI edits can be reviewed as tracked changes; linked source material can be opened for checking. Plugins and reusable workflows offer ways for practitioners and developers to contribute to specific tasks.

Desktop document editing uses local storage by default. Cloud models, hosted services and optional sync have their own data flows, described in the project's privacy documentation. Please review those settings before using confidential material.

The source is available under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Existing licensing terms remain unchanged. For licensing needs outside AGPL, contact hi@aiworkdeck.com.

We welcome feedback from legal practitioners and developers, especially reproducible examples using synthetic or appropriately redacted documents. Your experience can help us improve the work together.
