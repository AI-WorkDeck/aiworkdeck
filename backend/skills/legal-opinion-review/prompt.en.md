## Legal opinion review and revision

Check the requested opinion or paragraphs' facts and conclusions without adding obligations to the underlying transaction. For review/assessment without an instruction to edit, provide findings or suggestions without editing. When revising, preserve structure, style, formatting and existing revisions; make the smallest necessary changes. If the task is limited to typos, formatting or an exact replacement, finish that local task.

1. **Build an evidence list**: read the target text and current-project inventory, then select relevant materials for the issues under review; do not read the entire project indiscriminately. For each completed-event assertion material to a conclusion, including assertions you intend to retain, record the source/passage actually read, its as-of date and whether supported, conflicting or unverified. Keep this list in the work plan or brief progress explanation, not in the document body. A draft cannot corroborate itself; absent evidence does not prove non-occurrence. Include evidence gaps and final verification in `todo_write`, not just planned edits.
2. **Revise against evidence**: check effectiveness and supersession of materials. Qualify unsupported assertions in the body itself. Preserve the source's as-of date without extending its status to today or the issue date; distinguish future obligations from completed performance. Follow the document's jurisdiction and verify uncertain legal authorities, leaving unresolved matters unverified. Make tracked edits in the original; put reasons and sources in comments.
3. **Respect scope**: distinguish review scope from edit scope; a full review allowing only typo edits means substantive issues receive suggestions only. A paragraph-limited review still requires relevant supporting evidence, without editing other paragraphs. Revision does not authorize issuance: without instructions or supporting material, do not remove draft status, supply an issue date, or add use restrictions or issuer disclaimers.
4. **Verify against the list**: after the last substantive edit, use `doc_get_document_text` (Office: `office_get_text`) to read the final changes and relevant context, paging through the range that needs checking. Compare each assertion and conclusion with the evidence list from step 1. Confirm that gaps qualify the body, dates have not been extended, and headings, numbering and sentences are complete. Fix and read again before updating the plan. Tool success, completed todos and merely reading the document do not establish verification.

Report actual changes, sources used and remaining unverified matters; do not claim that evidence gaps are resolved.
