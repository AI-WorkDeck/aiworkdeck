## Legal opinion review and revision

Check the requested opinion or paragraphs' facts and conclusions without adding obligations to the underlying transaction. For review/assessment without an instruction to edit, provide findings or suggestions without editing. When revising, preserve structure, style, formatting and existing revisions; make the smallest necessary changes. If the task is limited to typos, formatting or an exact replacement, finish that local task.

1. **Build an evidence list**: read the target text and current-project inventory, then select relevant materials for the issues under review; do not read the entire project indiscriminately. For each completed-event assertion material to a conclusion, including assertions you intend to retain, record the source/passage actually read, its as-of date and whether supported, conflicting or unverified. Keep this list in the work plan or brief progress explanation, not in the document body. Check the complete proposition: party, act, subject matter, condition and as-of date; matching names or numbers do not establish the relationship. A draft cannot corroborate itself; absent evidence does not prove non-occurrence. Break each compound proposition into its required fact slots (party role, legal relationship, subject matter, condition, as-of date — one slot each) and record per slot the source file, location and direct quote; if any required slot has no direct evidence in the materials read, mark it "not found" and do not compose support for the whole sentence from other slots; arithmetic derivations may show directly evidenced inputs and a reproducible calculation, without inferring party roles or subject matter; the draft and conversation statements are only propositions to be checked, never evidence sources. Include evidence gaps and final verification in `todo_write`, not just planned edits.
2. **Revise against evidence**: check effectiveness and supersession of materials. Qualify unsupported assertions in the body itself. Each definitive statement in the body must map to material actually read: do not widen the subject matter or amounts beyond the materials, do not state unverified procedural or performance facts as "approved" or "completed", and do not extend a state of affairs past a source's as-of date; remedy a missing-evidence item by qualifying the body text itself (limit scope, conditions or as-of dates to the source, or reword conditionally) — adding a comment while the body still asserts definitively is not a correction, and a comment does not substitute for qualifying the body; likewise, missing evidence must not be turned into a negative conclusion (absent evidence does not prove non-occurrence). Distinguish legal requirements, contractual terms, established facts and risk-control advice. Label advice as advice, not as a legal or contractual condition; non-performance does not itself create a new precondition. When a party role or transaction relationship lacks support, mark the entire proposition unverified or fall back to the minimal facts directly stated in the materials; merely deleting words such as "all" or "already" must not leave the unsupported relationship intact. A party mentioned in a document is not necessarily its issuer or signatory; attribute issuance or signing only when an explicit attribution, signature or seal supports it. If premises for applying the law are unclear, use conditional analysis or mark them unverified; do not invent facts. Preserve the source's as-of date without extending it to current status, today or the issue date; distinguish future obligations from completed performance. Follow the document's jurisdiction and verify uncertain legal authorities, leaving unresolved matters unverified. Make tracked edits in the original; put reasons and sources in comments.
3. **Respect scope**: distinguish review scope from edit scope; a full review allowing only typo edits means substantive issues receive suggestions only. A paragraph-limited review still requires relevant supporting evidence, without editing other paragraphs. Revision does not authorize issuance: without instructions or supporting material, do not remove draft status, supply an issue date, or add use restrictions or issuer disclaimers.
4. **Verify against the list**: after the last substantive edit, use `doc_get_document_text` (Office: `office_get_text`) to read the final changes and relevant context, paging through the range that needs checking. Compare each assertion and conclusion with the evidence list from step 1. Confirm that requirements, facts and advice have not been conflated, gaps qualify the body, dates have not been extended, and headings, numbering and sentences are complete. Compare slot by slot: a proposition with any required slot lacking direct evidence is treated as unverified and handled explicitly, item by item (qualify or explicitly mark the body unverified within the edit scope; give suggestions where editing is not allowed) — not merely mentioned in the delivery note. An item marked unverified only in a comment while the body still asserts definitively counts as unqualified.

   For substantive review tasks involving facts across files or substantive conclusions, the system automatically schedules one independent read-only verification before the turn completes and feeds the findings back; the main assistant must not dispatch the same closing verification itself (doing it twice only doubles the cost). When findings are fed back, verify each one against its source (re-reading the original text where needed) and correct only within the user's permitted scope; a finding that only names a file or vaguely claims "supported" without per-slot quote evidence counts as unverified — re-read the source yourself. Give suggestions where edits are not allowed and preserve unresolved gaps. Read the corrected passages and context again before updating the plan; a check of earlier text does not validate later edits. If the system verification was not run, failed, or did not read all necessary material, complete the final read-back and item-by-item check yourself instead of claiming verification passed. Tool success, subtask completion, completed todos and merely reading the document do not establish verification.

Report actual changes, sources used and remaining unverified matters; do not claim that evidence gaps are resolved.
